## Also likely (short answers)

- **Do generic tool papers flood the list?** Before the judge, yes: the Linked lane is topped by SAMtools, BWA and STAR. The judge labels tool papers and the Lens moves them: down under Evidence, up under Method. (RANKING_AND_STORAGE 1.4)
- **Is the citation data reliable?** Not always. OpenAlex listed a 2017 review as cited by a 2016 seed, so a link counts only if the candidate is no more than a year newer than the seed, and the source link is shown so you can check. (RANKING_AND_STORAGE 1.4)
- **Where does my data go?** Seeds and the question go to the app, the model calls go to Token Factory, and the web queries go to Tavily. Notes stay in the browser (IndexedDB) and export as Markdown, BibTeX or CSL JSON. (RANKING_AND_STORAGE 4.1, 4.5)
